Public Pilot Study

Human+AI Decision Framework

A guided pilot study exploring how people make decisions with artificial intelligence and how their approach may change after a short introduction.

Approx. 30–35 minutes Voluntary Unpaid Participant code allowed
Start the pilot study

Please do not enter passwords, banking information, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

What you will do

  • Complete the study in the order shown.
  • Work through fictional decision scenarios.
  • Use AI as instructed during the study.
  • Answer brief reflection questions.

Privacy

You may use a participant code instead of your real name. Participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time.

Who can participate?

Adults interested in decision-making and AI. No specialist technical knowledge is required.

Purpose

This is an exploratory public pilot designed to test the Human+AI Decision Framework and improve the study before wider use.
